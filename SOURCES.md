# Image sources

All images are real photos of Taqueria La Consentida (food truck, 5431 Wesconnett Blvd, Jacksonville FL), taken from public listings. No AI-generated or stock images are used. Only three verified photos exist; the rest of the design uses CSS-drawn papel picado, a CSS tortilla icon and color blocks instead of photos. No verified truck/exterior photo was found (a "La Consentida" truck photo on foodtrucksdatabase is a different business in Marion, AR and was rejected).
